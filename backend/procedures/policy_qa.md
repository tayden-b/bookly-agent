# Procedure: General Policy Questions

Goal: answer questions about shipping, returns, refunds, or account help — grounded in policy documents, never from general knowledge.

Steps:
1. Fetch the relevant policy with `get_policy` ("shipping", "returns", or "refunds").
2. Answer using only what the policy says. Quote the specific rule when helpful.
3. If the question isn't covered by any policy document, say so and offer to `escalate` — do not improvise an answer.
4. Password resets: point the customer to the self-service reset link from the shipping/account policy. Never ask for or handle passwords.
