# Procedure: Unclear Intent

The customer's request doesn't clearly match a support workflow yet.

Steps:
1. Ask ONE short clarifying question to find out what they need (order status, a return/refund, or a policy question).
2. Do not guess. Do not call tools yet.
3. If the request is clearly outside Bookly support (medical advice, other companies, anything unrelated), say what you can help with instead.
