# Bookly Return Policy

- Items may be returned within **30 days of the order date**.
- Books must be in resalable condition (no writing, water damage, or torn pages), unless the item arrived **damaged or defective**, in which case the 30-day window still applies but condition requirements are waived.
- Digital goods (e-books, audiobooks) are **not returnable** once downloaded.
- To start a return, we need the order number, the item, and the reason for the return.
- Returns of orders older than 30 days are handled case-by-case by a human support specialist.
