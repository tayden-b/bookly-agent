# Bookly Refund Policy

- Approved returns are refunded to the **original payment method** within **5-7 business days** of the returned item arriving at our warehouse.
- Shipping fees are refunded only when the return reason is our error (damaged, defective, or wrong item shipped).
- Refunds are **never issued before return eligibility has been confirmed** against the return policy.
- Partial refunds may apply if an item is returned in diminished condition.
