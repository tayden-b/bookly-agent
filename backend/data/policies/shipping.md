# Bookly Shipping Policy

- **Standard shipping:** free on orders over $25, otherwise $4.99. Delivery in 3–7 business days.
- **Express shipping:** $12.99 flat. Delivery in 1–2 business days.
- Orders ship from our warehouse within 1 business day of being placed.
- Tracking numbers are emailed as soon as the carrier scans the package.
- **Password reset:** customers can reset their password at bookly.example/reset; support agents cannot see or change passwords.
